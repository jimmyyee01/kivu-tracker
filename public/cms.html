Website content

Pages are rendered server-side: the platform site at /p/<slug> and / for the front page, an account's content at /p/<account>/<slug> and /p/<account> for its landing page. Every page has a type (landing, help, terms, faq, custom) and an optional asset type; an account's help/terms pages are linked in that account's console. The content manager is a desktop console — it is not part of the mobile app.
