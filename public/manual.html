1 · What KIVU Tracker does

GPS trackers (vehicle units, the phone app, the built-in protocol gateways) send their position to this server over the air. The server decodes them, stores history, shows live state on the map, evaluates your alert rules and pushes notifications — while reports turn the history into trips, mileage and summaries you can print or export.

  • Live map with fleet status chips, geofences and the unit trail.
  • Speeding, harsh driving, geofence and no-data (offline) alerts.
  • Notifications by in-page bell, e-mail, Telegram and phone push.
  • Multi-account platform: each customer sees only their own fleet.

2 · Signing in and accounts

  1. Open the server address (or install the app) and press Sign in.
  2. Enter the username and password your administrator gave you.
  3. You stay signed in until you press Sign out — sessions renew themselves while you use the console.

Roles:

  • super — platform owner: every account, plans, platform settings.
  • admin — account owner: users, units, rules, billing for that account.
  • operator — daily work: monitoring, reports, geofences, alerts.
  • viewer — read-only: can watch and export, cannot change anything.

3 · The monitoring console

  1. Units panel (left): search by name/plate/IMEI, filter by status, click a unit — the map flies to it.
  2. Map: fleet status chips float on the map; click one to jump to that vehicle. Click the marker for speed, address, last fix, altitude, ignition and fuel where the device provides them.
  3. Details panel (right): live values, commands and the events list (position, speeding, geofence, offline, harsh…).
  4. Top bar: Monitoring, Reports, Administration, Billing, the alert bell, Install (desktop app), User manual and Sign out. On a phone, the red buttons hide/show panels to give the map full screen.

4 · Vehicle status explained

  • moving — speed above the moving threshold (default 5 km/h) or ignition on.
  • idle — ignition on, vehicle standing (real ignition bit from the device where available).
  • parked — no motion for a while, device still reporting.
  • offline — no data for longer than the online timeout (default 30 minutes); turns back automatically on the next fix.

Colour chips in the unit list and on the map always show the current state; the exact time of the last fix is under the marker details.

5 · Alerts and the notification bell

  1. The bell in the top bar shows a red badge with the number of unacknowledged alerts for your account (super admins: for all accounts).
  2. Open it to see each alert: reason, device, driver, account, time and location link. New alerts play two short beeps and vibrate the phone.
  3. Press Acknowledge all to silence the badge — acknowledgement is shared, so the whole team sees it was handled.
  4. The list refreshes every 20 seconds; with the app installed you also get a number badge on the desktop icon/task bar and in the title.

6 · Alert rules (e-mail, Telegram, push)

Administration → Notifications → New rule. Rule types:

  • offline — no data for N minutes; speed — over the limit (global, per-rule or geofence limit); geofence — enter/exit;
  • harsh — hard braking / rapid acceleration above the threshold (one per minute cooldown); plus ignition, SOS, power cut, fuel drop/fill and movement where the device supports them.

Delivery channels (per rule + platform-wide):

  • Bell — always, in the console.
  • E-mail — to the account's admins and the super admin (needs SMTP in Plans & billing).
  • Telegram — instant message to your phone; while an alert stays unacknowledged it repeats every 30 seconds (max 10 minutes), then stops.
  • Phone push — native banner at the top of the screen even when the app is closed; tap it to open KIVU (enable once: bell → Enable phone alerts).

7 · Adding a GPS device or the phone app

  1. Administration → Units → New. Choose the hardware type from the supported-device list (car scanners, bike, phone…).
  2. Enter the device's IMEI (or unique ID) exactly as printed on it.
  3. Power the device; when it reaches the server its state turns online and the marker appears. GT06 and EasyTrack devices connect to the public bridge port automatically.
  4. Optional: assign a driver, fuel/commands and a speed limit to the unit.

Phone as tracker: Get the app → install → ⋮ menu → Start tracker mode after entering the unit unique ID and device password — the phone then reports its own GPS like a hardware unit.

8 · Reports and exports

  1. Top bar → Reports.
  2. Pick the units, the date range and the report type.
  3. Generate — then print or export CSV (downloads are handed straight to the browser / phone).

The events list and audit trails live in the details panel and Administration, and can be filtered the same way.

9 · Administration

  • Users — create accounts, set roles, reset passwords, e-mail addresses (used for alert e-mails).
  • Units — devices, IMEIs, drivers, intervals, speed limits.
  • Drivers — names assigned to vehicles (shown in alerts).
  • Geofences — draw zones, set per-zone speed limits; enter/exit becomes an alert rule.
  • Notifications — the rules from section 6.
  • Settings — platform name, logo, theme, online timeout, moving threshold.

10 · Plans, billing and the super console

  • Billing (admins) — current plan, trial countdown, invoices; tracking pauses when a trial/plan ends but data stays readable.
  • Accounts (super) — create customer accounts, seed their alert rules, manage platform-wide settings.
  • Plans & billing (super) — prices, gateways and the SMTP host for outgoing alert e-mails.
  • Super admins automatically receive a copy of every account's alert e-mails and see every account's alerts in the bell.

11 · Mobile: install, push, badge

  1. Desktop: press Install in the top bar — KIVU opens as its own window without a URL bar, with the pin icon on the desktop/task bar and the alert count as a badge. (Needs the HTTPS address.)
  2. Android: Get the app → Download the APK → install (Android 6+). Log in once — the console remembers the server.
  3. Push: bell → Enable phone alerts (or use the app) — banners arrive instantly even when the app is closed; tapping opens KIVU.
  4. The app never signs you out unless you press Sign out.

12 · Troubleshooting FAQ

  • Device stays offline — check SIM/coverage, that the IMEI was entered exactly, and that the tracker is configured for this server's bridge port.
  • No e-mails — SMTP is not configured yet (super: Plans & billing). The bell/Telegram/push work regardless.
  • Phone silent — enable phone alerts in the bell, allow notifications for the site/app in the OS settings, and use the HTTPS address for the web version.
  • Map grey/banded — pan once or resize; tiles load per view.
  • Signed out unexpectedly — only Sign out or an administrator disabling your account ends a session; otherwise it renews itself.
  • Wrong e-mail on an alert — recipients come from the rule plus the account's admin users; fix addresses under Administration → Users.
